🇳🇵 Nepal Pulse — Data Deletion Instructions

Effective date: May 6, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of any data that the Nepal Pulse Facebook App may have accessed or stored in connection with your Facebook account.

What Data We Hold

The Nepal Pulse App is a private, administrator-only tool. It does not collect, store, or process personal data from Facebook users, followers, or visitors. The only data the App accesses is the Facebook Page Access Token of the Page administrator, used to publish posts on the Nepal Pulse Page.

How to Remove App Access

If you are the Page administrator and wish to revoke the App's access to your Facebook account and Page, follow these steps:

  1. Log in to your Facebook account.
  2. Go to Settings & Privacy → Settings.
  3. Click Apps and Websites in the left menu.
  4. Find Nepal Pulse (or the associated developer app) in the list.
  5. Click Remove to revoke all access immediately.

Revoking access invalidates the Page Access Token. No further data can be accessed or posted by the App after removal.
