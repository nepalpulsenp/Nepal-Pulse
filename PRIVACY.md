# Privacy Policy — Nepal Pulse News

**Last updated:** May 6, 2026

Nepal Pulse News ("we", "our", or "us") operates an automated news aggregation and publishing service that shares Nepal-related news on social media platforms.

## Information We Collect

Nepal Pulse News is an automated news bot. We do not collect, store, or process any personal information from users who read our posts on Facebook or other social media platforms.

## How We Use Information

- We do not sell, trade, or share any personal data.
- Our system only reads publicly available news sources (RSS feeds) and publishes summarized news content.
- No user data is collected from our social media followers.

## Third-Party Platforms

Our content is published on Facebook and other platforms. Please refer to their respective privacy policies for information on how they handle your data:
- [Facebook Privacy Policy](https://www.facebook.com/policy.php)

## Data Retention

We do not retain any personal user data. Our internal database only stores news article metadata (headlines, URLs, publication dates) from public news sources.
